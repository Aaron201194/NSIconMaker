# **NSIconMaker**


<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aaron201194/NSIconMaker/main/Icon.png" alt="NSIMLOGO" width="200">

A Profile Icon creator where you can build your own icons and share them in the vault!

Meant to mimic the switch's profile menu for familiarity.

**ALL ON THE WEB!**

# **Usage**

First, get your images made.

They're separated into 4 simple layers;

**Background**

**Avatar**

**Decoration 1**

**Decoration 2**

# **When Done**

Go into the editor and it's similar to the switch at first, click the pencil on your icon.

Then if you want an image for your background, type in "image".

After that, upload your images as they come.

*Layer1 = D1*

*Layer2 = D2*

# **After Creating**

You can click on "Save/Load" and get your images DataURL that the program uses to manage the images in a long string of numbers to cut down length.
You can save this anywhere

You can also hit "Finish/Download" to download a file perfectly compatible with GoldLeaf on your modded switch!

And if you want to, you can share it with everyone via the "Upload Icon Pack" button to share it inside the Vault!
Please fill everything out correctly, give it a name, give it an author, and try to give it a description as best as possible.
